# RoboQuo — Collaborative robot brand audit (2026-11-10)

**Scope:** Cobot manufacturer directory and model selector in development PR #9. This is a curated starting set, **not** a claim to enumerate all worldwide manufacturers.

- Entire robot directory: **148** brands
- Collaborative robot brands: **40**
- Verified manufacturer/product sites newly researched on 2026-11-10: UFACTORY, RealMan, DUCO, Flexiv, Productive Robotics, Standard Bots, Elephant Robotics, plus existing OMRON, Kawasaki, NACHI collaborative product pages
- No real seller listings, product stock, prices, payments, seller accounts, or production Supabase schema were changed.
- `official-source` means a product/manufacturer source was recorded, **not** that RoboQuo is an authorized dealer, verified reseller, or that any safety/compatibility claim has been validated.
- `directory` means a reference entry still requiring individual vendor/product verification before claims of current availability.
- A manufacturer may offer many robot types; inclusion in cobots means at least one relevant collaborative line.
- Robot and gripper (EOAT) vendors are kept separate. Collaborative safety suitability always depends on the installation risk assessment.
