05 / FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do measurements require internet?

Measurements over a local Ethernet connection to Compute Box do not require the internet. License issuance may use a separate process.

Does it support V2 and QC?

The app separates Compute Box LAN and USB DAQ modes. Connections and units vary by device, firmware and configuration, so hardware validation is required.

What happens after 30 days?

Existing measurement files are not deleted. New measurements and report generation are restricted after a trial expires.

Are license keys issued instantly?

Manual PC-bound license issuance is planned initially. Payments and automatic fulfillment are not connected yet, and final sale terms will be published before launch.

Is this an official OnRobot website?

No. This is a prelaunch store for an independent HEX diagnostics software project. It is not an official OnRobot A/S sales website.
