RoboQuo v24.4 — Trust, Photo-first UX, SEO and PWA

- Seller onboarding now requires a real image selection before continuing.
- Selected seller images are previewed and kept with local demo listings in IndexedDB.
- Project form examples are placeholders instead of submitted default values.
- Illustrative listings and quote panels are labeled DEMO and cannot be purchased.
- Verification, media and condition claims only appear when supported by listing data.
- Supabase authentication errors are mapped to all ten interface languages.
- Purchase-channel checks show relative and exact freshness dates.
- Canonical/social metadata, robots.txt, sitemap.xml, social preview and PNG PWA icons added.
- Dialog focus, ARIA labeling, keyboard navigation and decorative icons improved.
- Service-worker cache updates automatically without a hand-maintained version string.

Known production follow-ups:
- Replace hash routes with indexable listing/manufacturer routes.
- Move seller drafts and images to authenticated server-side storage.
